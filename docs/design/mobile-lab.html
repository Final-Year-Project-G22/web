Mobile palette lab — the consumer app's colors on the web admin

Read directly from mobile/DESIGN.md (M3 system): slate-navy primary #1E293B, emerald secondary #10B981 (growth/success), amber tertiary #F59E0B (cultural warmth), M3 slate surfaces, 12dp radius, elevation-over-borders, and the navy→blue hero gradient as their only ornament. Four treatments below — light + dark each — plus the Flag reference for comparison. Honest notes: navy+emerald is the most common SaaS palette family (distinctiveness risk), and M3 dark themes lighten the primary to pale slate #94A3B8 (unusual on desktop but exactly what the app ships).

The real tradeoff: the mobile trio (navy/emerald/amber) is the same product's identity — one design system across both apps is a genuine argument, and emerald's "growth" meaning is a perfect fit for a formalization admin. The cost: navy + emerald is the most common SaaS palette on earth (Linear, Vercel-adjacent, dozens of admin tools), so the web admin would lose some of the originality this effort is about — though the tibeb weave, stamps, Ethiopic numerals, and parchment/forest surfaces stay in every option. Pick: a number + mode, a mix ("2 light + 3 dark"), or stay with 4 (Flag). Then the chosen direction goes into the full prototype.
